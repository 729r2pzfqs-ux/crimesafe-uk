How Street Crime Data Works

Our street-level crime data comes directly from the UK Police API, which aggregates reports from 44 police forces across England, Wales, and Northern Ireland. Each street is assigned a safety score from 0 to 100 based on the volume and severity of reported incidents relative to similar areas.

Scores above 70 indicate lower-than-average crime rates, while scores below 30 suggest higher crime activity. Use the search below to find specific streets and compare safety across different areas.
