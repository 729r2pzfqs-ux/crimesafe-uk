Is Wakefield Central Safe?

Wakefield Central is a high-crime area with a safety score of 9/100, placing it in the bottom 91% of UK neighbourhoods. This area has significantly higher crime rates than most UK areas, with 536 crimes recorded in the most recent month.

Violent crime accounts for 16% of all incidents (84 out of 536). Wakefield Central ranks #4,489 out of 4,926 neighbourhoods in the UK.

We recommend extra caution, especially after dark. Avoid walking alone at night and keep belongings secure.
