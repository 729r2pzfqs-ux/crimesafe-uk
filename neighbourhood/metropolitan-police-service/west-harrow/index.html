Is West Harrow Safe?

West Harrow is a high-crime area with a safety score of 11/100, placing it in the bottom 89% of UK neighbourhoods. This area has significantly higher crime rates than most UK areas, with 547 crimes recorded in the most recent month.

Violent crime accounts for 8% of all incidents (46 out of 547). West Harrow ranks #4,369 out of 4,926 neighbourhoods in the UK.

We recommend extra caution, especially after dark. Avoid walking alone at night and keep belongings secure.
