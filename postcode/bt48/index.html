BT48 Postcode Area

Derry City and Strabane

About the BT48 Area

The BT48 postcode district covers the western part of Derry/Londonderry, Northern Ireland's second-largest city. It includes much of the historic Cityside within the old city walls, as well as surrounding residential areas. Derry is situated on the River Foyle and is the main urban centre of the Derry City and Strabane district council area.

BT48 falls within the Police Service of Northern Ireland (PSNI) jurisdiction. Unlike police forces in England and Wales, the PSNI does not publish neighbourhood-level crime data through police.uk. This means that granular, neighbourhood-mapped crime statistics are not available for this postcode district on CrimeSafe UK.

According to PSNI statistics, the Derry City and Strabane policing district records crime rates somewhat above the Northern Ireland average for anti-social behaviour and public order offences, but below average for burglary. Overall, the area has seen a general downward trend in recorded crime over recent years.

Why is there no neighbourhood data for BT48?

CrimeSafe UK uses data from police.uk, the official source of street-level crime data in England and Wales. The Police Service of Northern Ireland (PSNI) does not participate in the police.uk neighbourhood data programme, so BT postcode areas have limited or no neighbourhood-level crime data available on this site.

For crime statistics in Northern Ireland, you can visit the PSNI official crime statistics page, which provides detailed breakdowns by policing district. The nearby postcode areas listed above have some neighbourhood data available.
