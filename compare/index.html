Frequently Asked Questions

Is Compare Neighbourhoods — CrimeSafe UK or Compare Neighbourhoods safer?

Compare the safety scores above to see which area has lower crime rates based on official March 2026 data from data.police.uk.

How are safety scores calculated?

Scores range from 0-100 (100 = safest) and consider total crime volume, crime types, and area demographics using official UK police data.

How often is the data updated?

Crime data is updated monthly from the UK Police API. Our latest data covers March 2026.
