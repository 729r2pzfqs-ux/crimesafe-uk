Each city page shows a breakdown of crime across its neighbourhoods, with safety scores calculated from official UK Police data. Scores range from 0 (highest crime) to 100 (lowest crime), making it easy to compare areas within and between cities.

Whether you're considering a move, planning a visit, or just curious about your area, these statistics provide a data-driven picture of safety across the UK's major urban centres.
